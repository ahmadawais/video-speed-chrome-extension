# Privacy Policy

Video Speed does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data.

- **No network access.** The extension contains no code that makes network requests, and its pages use a Content Security Policy that blocks them.
- **No analytics or telemetry.** Nothing is measured or reported.
- **Local settings only.** Your preferences (shortcuts, site rules, last speed if you turn on "Remember speed") are stored in `chrome.storage.local` on your device. They are not synced to any account and are removed when you uninstall the extension.
- **Page access.** The extension runs on web pages only to find `<video>` and `<audio>` elements and respond to your shortcuts. It does not read page content or browsing history, or record which sites you visit.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/ahmadawais/videospeed/issues.
